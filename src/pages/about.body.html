Teaching methodology

From beads in the hand to answers in the head

Every Abacus level and Vedic Maths module follows the same four stages.

  1. 1

    Touch & count

    Children handle beads and blocks to feel what numbers mean.

  2. 2

    Guided practice

    Trainers model each bead move or sutra, then children practise with support.

  3. 3

    Visualise

    The abacus moves into the mind’s eye; sums are solved without the tool.

  4. 4

    Apply & solve

    Vedic shortcuts and timed rounds connect new skills to school maths.

Our trainers

The roles that guide every class

Each batch is led by a trainer in one of these roles, trained in the SutraBead method.

Abacus Trainer

Leads the Abacus levels — from first bead moves to fast mental calculation.

  • Teaches bead technique and finger movements
  • Runs visualisation and flash-number drills
  • Prepares learners for level assessments

Vedic Maths Trainer

Teaches the Vedic sutras and shows how each one helps with school maths.

  • Explains each sutra with worked examples
  • Links shortcuts to topics children meet at school
  • Builds habits of quick answer-checking

Junior Learning Facilitator

Guides our youngest learners (ages 4–6) through play-based number sense.

  • Uses songs, stories and bead games
  • Builds counting and number recognition
  • Keeps sessions short, lively and gentle

Online Class Trainer

Runs live online batches with the same levels as academy classes.

  • Teaches in real time — never pre-recorded
  • Checks bead work on camera, child by child
  • Helps families set up the abacus kit at home
