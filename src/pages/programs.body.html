Abacus Mental Arithmetic · Ages 4–12

Climb from touching beads to seeing them

Children start on a real soroban, then slowly close their eyes and calculate on an imagined one. The program runs in three levels: Basic, Intermediate and Advanced.

  • 2 classes a week · 90 min
  • Soroban kit included
  • Exam & certificate each level
Level 1 of 3

Basic

About 9 months

  • Bead values, place value and finger technique
  • Small & big friends for carrying and borrowing
  • 2–3 digit addition and subtraction

OutcomeAdds a column of five 2-digit numbers on the abacus in under a minute.

Vedic Maths · Ages 4–12

Sixteen sutras, three levels of mastery

One 75-minute class a week, linked to your child’s school syllabus. Pick a level to see what is taught.

Level 1 · about 3 months

Foundations & quick checks

Children learn to see numbers in parts and complements, so subtraction and addition become one-line steps.

  • Nikhilam — “all from 9 and the last from 10”
  • Fast addition, subtraction and digit sums
  • Casting out nines to check answers

Outcome: subtracts from powers of ten instantly and checks every answer.

Level 2 · about 5 months

Multiplication magic

Base methods turn tough multiplication into two small sums — the favourite level for most children.

  • Base 10, 100 and 1000 multiplication
  • Squares of numbers ending in 5
  • Vertically & crosswise for 2-digit numbers

Outcome: multiplies 2- and 3-digit numbers mentally in a single line.

Level 3 · about 3 months

Division, roots & exam edge

The sutras are applied to division, roots and simple algebra, with timed papers that mirror school exams.

  • Flag (straight) division
  • Square and cube roots of perfect powers
  • Simple equations and timed practice papers

Outcome: finds roots in seconds and finishes school papers with time to check.

How it works

From first visit to the next level

A simple, predictable rhythm — parents see progress at every step.

  1. Day 1

    Free 30-minute assessment

    A trainer checks number sense, focus and speed in a relaxed session.

  2. Week 1

    Level placement & batch choice

    We recommend the program and level, and you pick a centre or online batch.

  3. First class

    Kit, practice app & parent login

    Your child gets the abacus kit and worksheets; you get access to the parent dashboard.

  4. Every week

    Classes + 10 minutes a day

    Short daily practice at home, with monthly progress reports from the trainer.

  5. Every term

    Level exam, certificate, next level

    Children move up after each exam — and can enter academy competitions.
